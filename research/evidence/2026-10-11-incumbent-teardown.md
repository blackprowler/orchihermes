## 1. OrchidWiz

**Status: ALIVE — and mid-transformation.** Two products now coexist:

### 1a. Legacy desktop: OrchidWiz Encyclopedia 20.x (Windows)
- URL: https://www.orchidwiz.com/products/orchidwiz-encyclopedia (search snippet, fetched via Brave index 2026-10-11); https://www.orchidwiz.com/pages/downloads ; https://www.orchidwiz.com/pages/faqs (search snippets 2026-10-11)
- Product: "OrchidWiz Encyclopedia 20.0" / download page references **Encyclopedia 20.A** with **265,000 images, >12 GB** install (10 min–hours download). Minimum system requirements: **Windows 8 or Windows 10**, 14 GB disk. Windows-only (UpdateStar confirms: "runs on the following operating systems: Windows").
- Pricing: historical one-time ~$35 (pass-1 finding); storefront snippet showed **$69.99** for the current Encyclopedia (search snippet 2026-10-10); separate Quarterly Update & Maintenance subscription exists (FAQ snippet).
- Forum mention: NewHorizonOrchids forum "OrchidWiz is back?" (https://www.newhorizonorchids.com/forum/viewtopic.php?t=6788, snippet 2026-10-11): "version 20 is fully self-contained. Upgraded licenses can be transferred" — suggests a rebuild/relaunch of the desktop line aimed at prior customers.

### 1b. New web product: orchidwiz.org (fetched directly 2026-10-11)
- URL: https://orchidwiz.org/ and https://orchidwiz.org/user-guide (fetched in browser 2026-10-11; JS app rendered fine)
- Run by **International Orchid Foundation 501(c)(3)** (nonprofit) — footer contact admin@orchidwiz.org. User guide "Last updated Oct 2, 2026" — actively maintained.
- Price: **$9.99/month or $99.99/year**, 7-day free trial, no card needed.
- Feature list (from rendered user guide, 2026-10-11):
  - Full orchid profiles; **image-similarity search**; "Lindley Orchid AI" assistant (beta) that can edit your collection
  - **Hybridizer tools**: breeding & ancestry tools, **Reverse Fingerprint Matcher**, **Hybrids Advisor**, ancestry pedigree chart + full-screen family tree, **progeny/descendant lines**, "When your cross gets registered" notification
  - **My Collection**: plant records, photos, **bloom records**, Active label, Journal, Orchid Groups, archiving, **QuickAdd tag scanning**, **plant-tag printing**, import/export
  - **Awards database** (awards.orchidwiz.org, 127k+ awards per pass-1 snippet), Judges' Consensus scoring tool, AwardsDesk for registrars, reviewer program
  - Public collection pages with privacy controls ("Who can see your plants")
  - Multi-language (English/Spanish/German), community page, data-coverage page
- **No evidence yet** of: pollination log with pod/flask tracking, RHS registration form export, inventory/sales, seller→buyer record transfer. (User guide table of contents fetched — none of these named; see coverage gaps.)

### 1c. History & community signals (why users tolerate it)
- Timeline from forum threads (snippets 2026-10-11):
  - Ran ~17 years under Alex & Marianna; announced closure **Dec 31, 2022** (Slippertalk "OrchidWiz Being Retired" https://www.slippertalk.com/threads/orchidwiz-being-retired.52965/) — site made no mention, frustrating buyers (Slippertalk "OrchidWiz closing down"… forum NHO t=5597).
  - Came back: **Version 20.0 released ~2023-24, priced $250** ("complete reinstallation with updated data, not an update to 9.0" — https://www.slippertalk.com/threads/orchid-wiz-is-back.57439/).
  - **Sales of Encyclopedia discontinued Jan 1, 2025** (quoted by a user in r/orchids: "We have discontinued all sales of OrchidWiz Encyclopedia last January 1, 2025… planning to resume… new version in the second half of 2026" https://www.reddit.com/r/orchids/comments/1j1jxvy/orchidwiz/).
  - "After a 2 year break (they were not able to get someone to take over the business)" — Orchid Board thread (https://www.orchidboard.com/community/orchid-lounge/114545-orchidwiz.html, snippet 2026-10-11).
  - Legacy Desktop was reported not to support Windows 11 (r/orchids 154rhdm snippet). Encyclopedia 20.A page still lists Windows 8/10 as minimum (orchidwiz.com FAQ snippet) — **NEEDS VALIDATION whether the .com desktop line is actually selling in Oct 2026 or is a frozen catalog.**
- User base claim: 5,000+ users in 61 countries (pass-1 note, sourced from orchidwiz marketing). Data scale: 170k+ hybrids, 33k species, 127k awards, 265k images.
- Slippertalk reaction to the web version: "cheaper than before and the search is free!" (https://www.slippertalk.com/threads/orchidwiz-org.60523/, snippet 2026-10-11) — community is watching, sentiment cautiously positive on the pivot.
- **Why users tolerate it:** it is the only deep structured dataset (RHS-synced register + awards + parentage) with offline encyclopedia; switching cost is the 265k-image local library and journal; the legacy product's instability (retire/return ×2, broken zip export needing a "Repair Agent") is accepted because no alternative carries the same data.

### 1d. What OrchidWiz web (orchidwiz.org) does NOT have (user-guide TOC + pages fetched 2026-10-11)
- No **pollination/pod log** (no pod→flask→seedling workflow; breeding tools = ancestry analysis, Reverse Fingerprint Matcher, Hybrids Advisor, Judges' Consensus — planning/analysis, not a cross log).
- No **RHS registration export/submission** — the flow is the reverse: RHS registers, OrchidWiz notifies you to *link* the name (collection-registrations page). "Suggesting an unregistered hybrid" feeds OrchidWiz's own DB, not the RHS form.
- No commerce, no inventory/sales, no seller→buyer record transfer, no plant passport. Collections are personal; sharing = read-only public pages per plant/user.

## What we can still do that none of them does
1. **Seller→buyer record inheritance** (the transferable record itself): all three treat a record as owner-bound with read-only share links. Nobody issues a record that *moves with the plant* on sale/gift, with provenance chain (cutting-from-whom, TC status, reversion history) intact.
2. **RHS registration-ready export from a private cross log.** OrchidWiz consumes the register but never helps you file it; Velvet30/InPlantory ignore orchid registration entirely. The £12/registration incumbent offers zero workflow tooling (pass-1 §2).
3. **Orchid-native pod→flask→seedling/nursery inventory + sale attachment** (passports/CITES paperwork at shipping). Velvet30's Nursery Pro is the nearest concept and is unshipped, unpriced, and all-plant-generic.
4. **Cross-selling the wedge into aroids at the point of sale** — InPlantory/Velvet30 are collector-side journals; neither is present at the transaction.

(Assumption) OrchidWiz's nonprofit pivot makes it a data moat + distribution channel (import their users' journal via WIZJournal.7z / orchids.org sync) rather than a wedge competitor; its roadmap could add collection-sharing but its mission (encyclopedia/awards) and $9.99 consumer positioning point away from nursery commerce.

- OrchidWiz: **Medium** — strongest data + brand + nonprofit durability; overlaps collection tracking, but no transfer/registration-export/commerce. Can react faster than the legacy reputation suggests.
