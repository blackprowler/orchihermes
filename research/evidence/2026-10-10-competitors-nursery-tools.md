## 2. Hybrid / breeding records (orchid-specific)

### RHS International Orchid Register (the incumbent "system")
- URL: https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/horticulture-hub/plant-registration/orchid-hybrids (fetched 2026-10-10)
- Price: **£12 per new hybrid name**, payable after acceptance; +£12 for hard-copy certificate posted outside UK; digital certificate free. >4,000 new orchid registrations/year. Online portal (not mobile-enabled — third-party guide: https://herebutnot.com/how-do-you-register-a-new-orchid-hybrid-name/).
- Process: PDF/online application (https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/pdfs/plant-registration-forms/orchid-hybrid-registration.pdf — "registration fee of UK £12.00 payable once accepted"), grex-level registration of cross (pod parent × pollen parent); searchable register gives parentage of grexes.
- Target segment: any hybridizer worldwide; hobbyists included.
- Features vs H2: registration + public grex lookup only. **No private cross-log, no seedling/family tracking, no commerce.** It is the data source a new tool should sync to/from, not a competitor per se.

### OrchidWiz
- URLs: https://www.orchidwire.com/Software/1/index.html (fetched 2026-10-10); https://www.orchidwiz.com/collections/frontpage & https://www.orchidwiz.com/products/orchidwiz-encyclopedia (search snippets 2026-10-10; site is JS-rendered, direct fetch returned only shell)
- Price: was $35 one-time; new online version OrchidWiz.org "beta pricing" — storefront snippet shows **$69.99** (RHS-updated DB: 170k+ hybrids, 33k species, 127k awards, journal feature).
- Target segment: hobbyist orchid growers (claims 5,000+ users in 61 countries).
- Features vs H2: encyclopedia + personal journal; legacy desktop software; discontinued old sales Jan 2025 to rebuild online (Reddit r/orchids). **Collection records, not a nursery commerce + lineage system.**

### Velvet30
- URL: https://velvet30.com/ (fetched 2026-10-10 — JS app; content from structured data/meta)
- Price: **free during beta**; paid "Pro" subscription planned post-beta.
- Target segment: hobbyist/pro hybridizers — foliage & flower plants (Anthurium, Philodendron, Alocasia, Hoya, **orchids** named).
- Features vs H2: logs pollinations (pod/pollen/date/method), auto-built multi-generation pedigree trees, private-by-default with public share pages, breeder network. **Closest conceptual competitor to the lineage half of H2** — but no commerce, no nursery inventory, no plant passport. ## 3. Plant passport systems

### Rules
- EU plant passport **mandatory since 14 Dec 2019** under Plant Health Regulation 2016/2031 + Official Controls Reg 2017/625; required for movement of plants-for-planting between businesses, and (per practitioner account) for **online/distance sellers** selling directly to the public in Ireland: https://www.supplychainit.com/creating-legally-compliant-eu-plant-passport-labels-easily-in-your-web-browser/ (fetched); https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/plh4l/plant-passports (fetched). Content requirements: botanical name, botanical/plant name, traceability code, country of origin, sender's registration number (Supply Chain IT article).
- UK: separate UK plant passport scheme post-Brexit — professional operators selling **by distance contract** or B2B in GB/Isle of Man/Channel Islands must issue passports: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/issuing-plant-passports-to-trade-plants-in-great-britain (fetched 2026-10-10); https://aphascience.blog.gov.uk/2023-03-01/plant-passports/ (search 2026-10-10). Face-to-face retail exempt; issuing requires professional-operator registration + training (practitioner account: https://individualplantsnursery.com/blog/plant-passports-explained, search 2026-10-10).

### Software offering passport generation
- **Güse "EU Plant Passport Online"** (browser-based): template labels, 48,000 botanical-name DB, PDF/thermal-printer output — https://www.supplychainit.com/... (fetched). Price not published. Point tool: labels only — no inventory, no lineage.
- **Linix** coding/marking systems for passports: https://www.linxglobal.com/en/solutions/coding-types/plant-passport/ (search 2026-10-10) — industrial marking, not SMB software.
- **Graffico** nursery management software (IT): advertises "Lot management with supplier origin, arrival date, and **associated plant passport**" + integrated e-commerce — https://graffico.it/en/solutions/nursery-garden-center-management-software (fetched 2026-10-10). But it's **custom/tailor-made development, quote-based** — not a product. This is the only fetched vendor combining passport + e-commerce, and it's bespoke → strong signal of an unmet productized need.
- Sunfire POS / Rapid Garden POS: tag/label printing from inventory DB (https://www.sunfirepos.com/garden-center-pos-nursery-tag-printing-integration.html; https://rapidgardenpos.com/printing/) — labels yes, **no evidence of EU-passport compliance fields** (NEEDS VALIDATION).

## 4. E-commerce for plant stores / orchid marketplaces

- **Shopify** is the de-facto platform for small orchid specialty stores (e.g., Needmore Orchids on myshopify.com — https://needmore-orchids.myshopify.com/; Shopify orchid theme page https://themes.shopify.com/websites/orchids). Generic: no per-plant lineage, no passport fields.
- **AOS Orchid Marketplace** (curated vendor directory, links out to vendors; promoted to 100k+ growers): https://marketplace.aos.org/about-us — directory, not a transactional marketplace.
- **OrchidWire** — vendor/listing directory incl. software: https://www.orchidwire.com/Vendors/1/United_States.html — no integrated sales tooling.
- Society "AOS on-line auctions" run via society sites (e.g., https://cflorchidsociety.org/event/aos-on-line-auction/) — ad-hoc, no vendor tooling.
- **Orchid LLC / Orchid POS / Orchid eCommerce** is a *firearms* FFL POS company — name collision only, irrelevant (https://orchidadvisors.com/best-ffl-ecommerce-website/, fetched).
- OrchidPro = AOS judging research tool, not commerce (https://www.aos.org/awards-judging).

## Gaps observed
1. **No fetched product combines commerce + plant-level lineage + crossing records + passport generation.** Nursery ERPs (ET Grow, Argos, SBI, Mprise) do inventory/wholesale from ~$4k+/yr; low-end tools (Small Grower Software $19/mo, generic POS) do batches/labels; Velvet30 does pedigree but no store; Güse does passport labels but no store; Graffico bundles passport+e-comm only as bespoke projects.
2. **"Missing middle" is documented by a competitor**: Small Grower Software's own guide frames backyard/small nurseries as underserved between spreadsheets and $4k ERP (fetched) — and orchid-specific needs (per-plant clonal history, grex data) sit even further below even that.
3. **Hybridizer workflow is register-at-the-end, log-in-spreadsheet**: RHS charges only £12/name and offers zero ongoing record-keeping; OrchidWiz is mid-rebuild (old product discontinued Jan 2025); Velvet30 (beta, free, no orchid focus depth, no commerce) is the only modern pedigree tool. A tool that seeds grex data from RHS/OrchidWiz and exports registration-ready forms has no incumbent moat.
4. **Plant passport generation for distance sellers is a regulatory must with almost no SMB software** — Güse is label-only, Graffico bespoke. Orchid nurseries selling EU/UK online (incl. tissue-culture flasks/seedlings = plants-for-planting) need passport fields tied to lot/batch — natural feature for H2's product.
5. **Orchid e-commerce runs on generic Shopify** with no clonal-lineage display ("parentage: Pod × Pollen, registered grex") and no passport attachment at checkout/shipping.
