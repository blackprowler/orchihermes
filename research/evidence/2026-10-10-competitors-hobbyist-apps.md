## 4. Community & plant-history handover (the key differentiator)

- Community: Greg has the strongest general community (botanicaly.com roundup snippet). PlantNet has citizen-science photo contributions. OrchidKeeper offers social-media export (collages/yearbooks), not in-app community. No orchid app found with a feed, forum, or shared profiles.
- **Full-history handover**: exactly ONE precedent found — Orchid Journal's "Gift an orchid" bundles a plant's history+photos into a shareable file (local file, no QR, no ongoing link, both parties outside any platform). Orchid Keeper has a "Plant Tag Scanner" (tag→record) but its listing gives no evidence tags are scannable by a *new owner* to inherit the record — NEEDS VALIDATION (test the app).
- QR-tag ecosystem check (web_search 2026-10-10): qrlog.app/acemaker and joyofplants.com sell QR plant labels linking to a *personal* garden log owned by the label creator; none is transferable to a buyer/new owner as a product. qr-code-generator.com blog treats QR inventory as nursery-side only.
- No app found supports: seller→buyer handover of care history at purchase, shared collection profiles, or illness/bloom history that travels with the plant.

## Gaps observed

1. **Nobody owns orchid collection management.** Orchid Album ($7.99 one-time, 2014, last update Jan-2025) is the community default yet has no ID, no cloud, no sync, no sharing, no modern UX. The 2026 orchid-app wave (Orchid Keeper, OrchidManager, Orchid Journal, Orchid Water & Rebloom — all launched Mar–Sep 2026) validates demand but all are solo-dev, near-zero ratings, mostly free.
2. **History handover is essentially unbuilt.** Only Orchid Journal's offline "gift file" touches it; no QR-tag + journal + ongoing-record transfer exists for adopted/bought plants anywhere, general or orchid. This is the clearest whitespace for hypothesis 1.
3. **Vendor/nursery side missing entirely.** Leafolio records vendors and orders, but nothing connects a nursery's records to the buyer's app (nurseries have no tool to publish care history that a buyer inherits). QR label vendors (qrlog, joyofplants) stop at the original owner.
4. **Monetization split**: general apps converged on $30–80/yr subscriptions (growli, verified Jul-2026); orchid apps are one-time $4.99–7.99 or freemium ~$40/yr — no one has proven SaaS pricing in orchid-specific collection management. Willingness to pay for *collector depth* (Leafolio breeding premium) vs *care reminders* (saturated, nearly free) is the pricing question. NEEDS VALIDATION via interviews.
5. **Collection tools are generic.** The 2026 cataloging wave (GreenFolio, VLH, Plant Archive, Leafolio) proves collectors want inventory + provenance, but none models orchid-specific data (genus/hybrid/registered cross, mount vs pot, media, skew/blooming cycles).
6. Earlier-run claims corrected: Ploi is **not** free/no-subscription (Ploi Pro IAP $5.99/$59.99 per App Store listing); **MiIvy could not be found at all**; PlantSnap pricing remains unverified (JS-only page).
