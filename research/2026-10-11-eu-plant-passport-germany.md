# EU Plant Passport for a German Orchid Seller — Rules, Process, Time, Cost

Research date: 2026-10-12 (UTC). Scope: Regulation (EU) 2016/2031 + Delegated Reg. (EU) 2019/2072 as applied in Germany; CITES interplay via BfN. Convention: every claim = URL + fetch date; opinions under **Assumption**; direction-changing claims flagged **NEEDS VALIDATION**.

## What is CLEAR

### 1) Does an orchid seller need a plant passport at all?
- Passport duty for intra-EU movement of regulated plants: Art. 78 ff. Reg. 2016/2031; the duty-relevant goods list is delegated to **Annex XIII of Implementing Reg. (EU) 2019/2072** (Art. 79). Sources (all fetched 2026-10-12): LTZ Augustenberg https://ltz.landwirtschaft-bw.de/,Lfr,W-2/Startseite/Arbeitsfelder/Pflanzenpass ; Regierungspräsidium Gießen https://pflanzenschutzdienst.rp-giessen.de/pflanzengesundheit/pflanzenpass/ ; ADD RLP Anlage 4 PDF https://add.rlp.de/fileadmin/add/Abteilung_4/Pflanzenschutz/Pflanzengesundheit/Registrierungspflichten/4_Anlage_Pflanzenpass.pdf
- **The exemption the brief attributes to "Art. 72" is actually Art. 81** ("Ausnahmeregelung für die direkte Lieferung an Endnutzer", verified in EUR-Lex consolidated text 02016R2031, https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/DE/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02016R2031-20191214, fetched 2026-10-12): direct supply to an end user (incl. hobby growers) needs **no** passport — but the exception **does not apply if the end user receives the plants by distance sale (Fernabsatz)** or in protected zones. Exact quote retrieved: "Für die Verbringung von Pflanzen … die direkt an einen Endnutzer, einschließlich Hobbygärtner, geliefert werden, wird kein Pflanzenpass benötigt. Diese Ausnahme gilt nicht für a) Endnutzer, die die betreffenden Pflanzen … im Fernabsatz erhalten …"
- **Consequence for our scenario (rule-level, clear): a German orchid seller shipping online (own shop/Etsy/eBay) — even to private hobby buyers — must put a plant passport on the shipped trade units.** Local on-site sales (market, nursery counter) are passport-exempt.
- **Orchids are covered**: Annex XIII covers *all* plants for planting ("zum Anpflanzen bestimmte Pflanzen", incl. young plants/cuttings, finished potted goods), seeds only for listed genera; no Orchidaceae exclusion appears on any authority page checked (LTZ: "Pflanzenpasspflichtig sind grundsätzlich alle zum Anpflanzen bestimmte Pflanzen"; LTZ URL above, fetched 2026-10-12). Orchidaceae appear in Reg. 2019/2072 mainly on the import side (Annex XI: cut flowers of Orchidaceae from third countries need a phytosanitary certificate — EUR-Lex page text, fetched 2026-10-12). Orchidaceae are **not** priority plants and no orchid RNQP is known — **NEEDS VALIDATION** against Annex IV/X of 2019/2072 full text (annex text could not be fully machine-read this pass; browser/EBANX blocks).
- Registration duty mirrors it: operators moving passport-required goods must register (Art. 65); businesses selling **only** directly to end users are registration-exempt, "dies gilt jedoch nicht für Versandhandel" — mail-order/online sellers must register. Source: LWK Niedersachsen (webcode 01034975) https://www.pflanzenschutzdienst-niedersachsen.de/pflanzenschutz/news/34191_Pflanzengesundheitliche_Regelungen_auf_einen_Blick (fetched 2026-10-12).

### 2) Process in Germany where a passport IS needed
1. **Authority**: the Pflanzenschutzdienst of the Bundesland where the business is headquartered (RP Gießen, URL above: "Der zuständige Pflanzenschutzdienst ist der Pflanzenschutzdienst des Bundeslandes, in dem das Unternehmen den Hauptsitz hat"). In practice per state: Bayern → LfL Institut für Pflanzenschutz (https://www.lfl.bayern.de/ips/pflanzengesundheit/024692/index.php, fetched 2026-10-12); Niedersachsen → LWK; Hessen → RP Gießen; Brandenburg → ISIP/LLV; Hamburg → Behörde (FAQ PDFs below). BMEL sets law, JKI is the national coordination/documentation body (Leitfaden für Pflanzenpassaussteller, FAQ: https://pflanzengesundheit.julius-kuehn.de/, fetched 2026-10-12) — neither issues the registration itself.
2. **Registration (Art. 65) + authorization ("Ermächtigung", Art. 89) in one step**: submit the Registrierungsantrag incl. annex listing your goods (Orchidaceae) to the state service; the Ermächtigung to issue passports yourself is requested within the registration (RP Gießen URL above; ADD RLP: "Diese Ermächtigung ist im Rahmen der amtlichen Registrierung mit zu beantragen" https://add.rlp.de/themen/landwirtschaft-und-weinbau/pflanzenschutz/pflanzengesundheit/pflanzenpass-und-handel-im-binnenmarkt , both fetched 2026-08… → fetched 2026-10-12).
3. **Conditions for Ermächtigung (Art. 89 + Delegated Reg. (EU) 2019/827)**: demonstrable knowledge of quarantine/RNQP pests, symptoms and prevention; documented procedures; staff training. (ISIP Brandenburg, https://www.isip.de/brandenburg/pflanzenschutzdienst/pflanzengesundheitskontrolle/pflanzenpasspflicht-390566 , fetched 2026-10-12.)
4. **Duties of an authorized issuer (Art. 90)**: risk-based inspections of plants at the right time on-site and in the immediate surroundings; documented internal controls; traceability records for every trade unit (in/out), kept ≥3 years (Art. 69; ISIP URL above).
5. **Label format (Art. 83 + Annex VII; DVO (EU) 2017/2313)**: rectangular, clearly separated label; word "Pflanzenpass/Plant Passport" top-right, EU flag top-left; **A** = botanical name (+optional cultivar); **B** = issuer's registration number; **C** = traceability code; **D** = origin country (ISO code). The traceability code may be omitted only for ready-to-sell finished goods for end users with low pest risk — note **DVO (EU) 2024/2507 extended the must-have-code list from 01.07.2025** (Lavandula, Salvia rosmarinus etc. — orchids not in that extension per LfL page above, fetched 2026-10-12).
6. **Who issues**: normally the authorized entrepreneur self-issues per unit; the authority can also issue on request (Art. 84) — the Bund deutscher Baumschulen notes authority-issued passports come "gegen Gebühr" (https://www.zgg-service.de/informationen/eg-pflanzenpass/ , fetched 2026-10-12).
7. **Validity/traceability**: passport accompanies the trade unit (one passport per Handelseinheit; each split consignment gets a new passport — LK NRW "Der neue EU-Pflanzenpass" PDF https://www.landwirtschaftskammer.de/landwirtschaft/pflanzenschutz/psd/pdf/pflanzenpass.pdf , fetched 2026-10-12). Replacement allowed under Art. 93(3) with traceability kept (ISIP URL above).
8. **Digital passport**: no German pilot found this pass. **UNKNOWN / NEEDS VALIDATION**: status of the Commission's Art. 96 experimental digital-passport arrangements (a 2024 Commission act is rumored in industry chatter; not confirmed from EUR-Lex this session — searches only surfaced the unrelated ESPR Digital Product Passport Reg. 2024/1781). Paper labels are the de-facto German practice.

### 3) CITES is a SEPARATE regime (do not mix with the passport)
- All ~30,000 orchid species (and hybrids) are CITES-listed; BfN: "Grundsätzlich unterliegen alle Orchideen … der CITES Genehmigungspflicht" — https://www.bfn.de/orchideen (fetched 2026-10-12).
- **Different authority, different purpose**: plant passport = plant health (state Pflanzenschutzdienst); CITES documents = species protection at EU external borders, issued **only by the Bundesamt für Naturschutz (BfN)** via citesantrag.bfn.de (https://www.bfn.de/regelungen ; Kiel service page, both fetched 2026-10-12).
- **Exemptions matter for a nursery**: seed, pollen, in-vitro flasks of artificially propagated plants, cut flowers of artificially propagated plants, and (under conditions) artificially propagated hybrids of *Cymbidium, Dendrobium, Phalaenopsis, Vanda* are **exempt from CITES permit duty** (BfN orchideen page, fetched 2026-10-12) — i.e., a mass-market Phalaenopsis nursery may never touch CITES paperwork, while a species seller shipping non-exempt specimens outside the EU always needs an Ausfuhrgenehmigung (Appendix A also has domestic controls — depth not verified this pass).
- **Fees (BfN, official Gebührenanlage to the Bundesgebührenordnung, PDF fetched 2026-10-12)**: Ausfuhrgenehmigung 1–4 positions **€44.75**; 5–20 positions €39.45; Wiederausfuhrbescheinigung 1–4 positions **€35.60**; certification per §33 VwVfG €11.20. Source: https://www.bfn.de/sites/default/files/2025-11/Anlage_Geb%C3%BChren_LIV_Ausfuhr_NEU_pac_0.pdf (overview page https://www.bfn.de/themen/cites/regelungen-rechtsgrundlagen/kostenverordnung.html ).
- **Processing time**: statutory 4 weeks when complete (Hessian service portal: "gesetzliche Bearbeitungsfrist … vier Wochen", recommended lead time 4–6 weeks — https://verwaltungsportal.hessen.de/en/leistung?leistung_id=B100019_580632 , fetched 2026-10-12).
- Intra-EU trade of Appendix-B orchids needs no CITES document, but proof of legal propagation/acquisition is required for the "artificially propagated" claim — practical depth **UNKNOWN** this pass.

## Implication for product

**Assumption / NEEDS VALIDATION framing**: For a German orchid micro-seller selling online, the passport is *legally mandatory* (Fernabsatz loophole is closed — this is the single most important rule-level finding) but *operationally small*: one-time registration + self-issued label + traceability log. That profile = mild, recurring compliance nuisance rather than acute pain — passport tooling looks more like a **vitamin** than a **painkiller** by itself.

The painkiller hypothesis only survives if one of these validates:
- **NEEDS VALIDATION**: micro-sellers get fined/pressured (marketplace or authority) often enough that "compliance-ready in 10 minutes" is worth paying for — no German enforcement evidence found this pass; enforcement appears CITES- and platform-driven, not passport-driven.
- **NEEDS VALIDATION**: the *fear/uncertainty* about the rule (which our own seller-mining shows: peer-to-peer compliance knowledge, Etsy threads) is strong enough that a "does my sale need a passport + generate the label + keep the Art.-69 log" generator is a wedge/trust feature inside a broader seller record tool — plausible, since the label fields (botanical name, registration no., traceability code, origin) are exactly record-system fields we already model.
- If sellers mostly sell locally (market/trade shows), they need nothing at all — segment check needed before betting on H2's passport feature.

Complementarity note: passport + CITES proofs + true-to-name records are the three compliance artifacts of the orchid trade; none of the incumbents (spreadsheets, OrchidWiz, generic nursery ERP — incumbent teardown 2026-10-11) covers them.
