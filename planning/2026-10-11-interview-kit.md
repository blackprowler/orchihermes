# Validation Interview Kit (R5) · Draft for owner review

Date: 2026-10-11 · Status: DRAFT — Illia reviews/edits before first use
Goal: crack the one thing evidence can't — **willingness to pay** (see GLOSSARY) — across the three candidate segments, and test the compliance-attached-record door-in (landscape note addendum).

## Rules of the interview (The Mom Test, condensed)
- Ask about **past behavior**, never hypothetical futures ("would you pay" → worthless; "what did you pay last time" → gold).
- Talk ≤ 30%, listen ≥ 70%. Dig every time you hear emotion or a workaround.
- Record notes within 10 min; tag quotes with segment + date for the evidence folder.
- 5–7 interviews per segment is the usual signal point; stop earlier if answers repeat verbatim.

## Recruiting channels
| Segment | Where |
|---|---|
| Collector-hobbyist (20+ plants) | r/orchids posts asking to be interviewed (check sub rules), local orchid society meetings, Instagram accounts posting collections |
| Hobby breeder | AOS society groups, OrchidBoard, Slippertalk; anyone who has registered a grex (public RHS data → you can find registrants!) |
| Small seller/nursery | Etsy orchid shop owners (the takedown threads name people with skin in the game), orchid Facebook group admins, eBay orchid sellers, AOS Commercial Council members |

Warm angle for sellers: ask about the **Etsy CITES takedown wave** — it's their loudest pain and a flattered-invitation opener. Do NOT pitch the product in the first 20 minutes.

## Script A — Collector-hobbyist (15 min)
1. Walk me through your collection: how many, where do they live (room/greenhouse), what do you pay roughly per year?
2. How do you keep track today? (Show me — screen/photo if remote.) What broke or annoyed you in the last month?
3. Tell me about the last plant you bought second-hand or adopted. What history did you get? What did you wish you had?
4. Have you ever tried an app for this? Which, how long, why did you stop? *(Orchid Album / Excel / Leafolio all expected)*
5. Have you paid for anything to help with records — templates, apps, journals? How much?
6. Last time you wanted to SHOW someone your collection — what did you actually send them?
**Listening for:** paid-for-workarounds (real WTP), history-loss stories, showing/sharing behavior.

## Script B — Hobby breeder (20 min)
1. How many crosses do you make per year? What do you record per cross, where?
2. Walk me through your last registered grex: how did the RHS form process go? What did it cost, start to finish?
3. Do you use OrchidWiz? (Why/why not — mention the $9.99/mo web version; what would make it worth it / not?)
4. Have you ever lost or been unable to reconstruct a cross/pod record? What did that cost?
5. Do you sell/distribute seedlings or flasks? How do buyers know what they're getting?
6. If a tool logged a cross and spat out the ready-to-submit RHS form + pod-to-seedling lineage: what would that be worth per year — €0 / 50 / 100 / 200? *(pick a ladder; push for "what have you paid for tools before")*
**Listening for:** registration frequency (recurring pain), OrchidWiz attitudes, price ladder honesty.

## Script C — Small seller / nursery (20 min)
1. Tell me about the Etsy takedown / cross-border policy experience (or: how do you handle CITES + phyto paperwork today, per order?)
2. How do you track inventory of clones/divisions and their parent plants? Show me.
3. How often do buyers ask "what's the history / is it true-to-name / virus-free"? How do you answer now?
4. What have takedowns or shipping losses cost you in the last year?
5. What tools do you pay for now (POS, Shopify, spreadsheets)? Total per month?
6. If your listing carried a verified plant record a buyer could inherit — does that sell more plants? Did you ever pay for anything that "sells more plants"?
**Listening for:** compliance labor-hours (the door-in!), trust artifacts improvised today, existing tool spend as WTP anchor.
