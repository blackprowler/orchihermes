# Strategy Review — is the transferable-record thesis still feasible?

Date: 2026-10-26 · Status: owner decision support · Author: Hermes (reasoning over tracks A–D, R1–R3, R8, WTP-mining 2026-10-26) · **PROPOSED decision, pending owner sign-off**

## 1. The strategic question restated

Not "is the pain real" (it is — strongest-evidenced thing we have) but: **is there a payer, at a survivable price, reachable at a survivable size?** Everything else is downstream of that.

## 2. What changed the picture since 2026-10-11

1. **OrchidWiz is alive, nonprofit, $99.99/yr — on a base of ~1,000 paying users** (WTP-mining §1, vendor's own words). This is the single most important strategic number we have: the entire demonstrated *breeder-tooling* paying market is on the order of **~$100k/yr revenue total, for all incumbents combined**. It proves people pay; it also proves the niche is *small by revealed preference*.
2. **Hobbyists pay for care/ID, never for records** (WTP-mining §2): convergent cancel-sentiment at £54/yr, "stupid expensive", spreadsheet fallback even among Planta subscribers. Ceiling $0–40/yr and falling, with the 2026 solo-dev orchid-app wave (4 launches, near-zero traction *at free*) as the supply-side proof that "built ≠ wanted" at this tier.
3. **Sellers' painkiller is compliance, not records** (R3 + R8): takedowns are the loud pain, but enforcement is rare and passport-alone is a Vitamin (R8 verdict). Records must *ride along* with something sellers already buy.
4. **Cross-genus replication of the pain** (Watch 2026-10-26): Hoya community shows the identical pattern — but presumably also the identical WTP ceiling. Genus breadth multiplies pain, not necessarily payers.

**(Assumption)** S4 is the only shape where the payer, the pain, and the moat (propagation-chain data that compounds with every transfer) sit in the same place. It is also exactly the H1∩H2 "meeting in the middle" the pass-1 synthesis called the strongest structural gap — now with a pricing logic attached.

## 4. Should we look for something else instead?

Honest answer: **no — not yet.** The current thesis is the best of a scored field (D-ranking: every alternative niche scored emptier = worse), and our *newest* evidence (WTP bounds) sharpens it rather than killing it — it kills S1/S2 framings inside the thesis and pushes us to S4. Searching for a new idea now would be fleeing a validation problem, not an idea problem. Two exit conditions are defined instead (see §6): if the kill-shot checks fail, the workspace pivots to R9 divergence or a new domain — that decision then, not pre-emptively.

The real risk named plainly: **this whole space may be a $100k/yr niche wearing a SaaS costume.** If R4 sizing + R7 forensics + smoke-test interest all come back weak, "not a venture product" is a legitimate, evidence-backed finding — better found now than after a build.

## 5. Kill-shot checks before any build or sign-off (cheap, mostly agent-runnable)

1. **R7 Marketplace forensics** (agent): do €100+ listings that carry provenance/lineage sell faster or at premiums? How do sellers claim "true-to-name" today? → tests S4's core claim. **Next queued item.**
2. **R4 Segment sizing** (agent): r/orchids vs r/aroids vs seller-group sizes; RHS registrant actives; Etsy orchid-shop counts → tests "survivable size" honestly.
3. **R10 Competitor graveyard** (agent): did past orchid apps die from no-traction or no-monetisation? → calibrates naive optimism.
4. **Smoke test** [owner]: one landing page for the S4 pitch ("sell your rare plants with a verifiable lineage card buyers keep"), 2 weeks + minimal promo in 1–2 communities; measure email captures per visitor. Owner action: deploy + promote. Nothing above this line gates on it.
5. **R5 interviews** [owner]: the interview kit is drafted; 5 breeder + 5 seller conversations remain the only true WTP gate. Suggested sequencing: after smoke test so interviews start from reactions to a concrete artifact.

## 6. Exit conditions (pre-registered, so the sunk-cost conversation happens now)

- **Kill S4 → R9 divergence or new domain** if: R7 finds no evidence that provenance claims affect sale outcomes at high price points AND smoke-test capture rate <~1–2% AND interviews (5+) show sellers wouldn't pay anything at the point of listing.
- **Downgrade to micro-business framing** (honest lifestyle product, no growth ambitions) if: payers exist but R4 sizes the total reachable market under ~$250k/yr.
- **Escalate to build-ready** if: R7 positive + smoke test ≥5% capture + ≥2 of 5 sellers in interviews state a concrete price they'd pay.
