# Orchinest Research

Commercial research workspace for a product/service serving **orchid hobbyists** and **small- to mid-size orchid nurseries**.

## Purpose
Three workstreams, kept in separate folders:

1. **Market & product-market-fit research** (`research/`) — segments, pain points, competitors, willingness to pay, evidence.
2. **Idea brainstorming** (`ideas/`) — product concepts, feature candidates, wild ideas. Diverge freely here; converge only via evidence.
3. **Project & scope planning** (`planning/`) — scope drafts, MVP definition, roadmaps, business-model notes.

## Conventions
- Every research note states its **source** and **date**; opinions go under an explicit "Assumption" heading.
- File naming: `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md` for dated notes; topic-only names for living documents.
- `research/evidence/` holds raw material (interviews, survey exports, scraped data); distilled conclusions go in top-level notes.
- Decisions get recorded in `planning/decisions.md` as: date, decision, rationale, revisit-when.
- Nothing in this workspace ships code; code lives elsewhere when the project graduates from research.

## Working agreement for agents
- Prefer primary sources (nursery owner interviews, forum threads, marketplace data) over generic market reports.
- When summarizing competitor landscapes, include price points and target segment, not just feature lists.
- Flag any claim that would change product direction as "NEEDS VALIDATION".
